## Verdict

Every stage is linear in its input: p stays between 0.81 and 1.14, and no family is flat, so each benchmark measures work that grows. The one exception is `clean`, whose p of 0.54 between 200 B and 2 KB is fixed per-call cost from its seven `replace` passes. The A/A pair is the important result. The code is identical, yet 15 of 18 rows say "faster" by up to 52%, so local wall time on this machine, with other agents compiling, cannot detect a regression smaller than about half. These rows are noise, not a change. Regressions are judged on CodSpeed's CPU simulation in CI (ADR 0012); local runs answer "roughly how long" and "does it scale".

In absolute terms, one search with a warm cache pays about 23 ms of `catalog/search` per 16k-entry catalog and 7 ms to load it, against network latencies of 0.1 to 1.5 s (`docs/synthesis/bench-2026-10-06.md`). No stage is worth optimizing yet.

## Caveats

- The first run had 1-minute load between 12 and 39 at 3% battery while charging. The second had load 5 falling to 4. Neither was a quiet machine; the medians above are upper bounds.
- The tree was dirty with docs only (`docs/`), so commit cabd593 identifies the measured code.
- Synthetic inputs match the real responses in shape and size, not in word distribution; real titles repeat words more often, which favors the catalog search's early exits.
- No instruction-count witness was taken locally (`assets/apple_instructions.rs`): CodSpeed's simulation in CI is the layout-insensitive witness for this repository.
