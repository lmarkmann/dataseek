# Bench, 2026-10-06

`dataseek bench` with its default queries ("climate temperature", "image classification", "unemployment rate", "gene expression cancer", "air quality", "population census"), 10 results asked per source, cache bypassed for live sources, catalogs warm. Run from Germany on a home connection; one run, so treat single-digit differences as noise.

How to read it: `answered` is queries answered out of attempts; `results` is the sum over six queries; `contributed` is the merged hits the source took part in; `unique` is the merged hits no other source found; the last column is the source sharing most hits, with the count, or the reason a source did not run.

Totals: 2,463 results, 2,355 unique hits, 2,417 contributions. About 4% of hits were found by more than one source, so the sources are complementary rather than redundant. Local catalogs answer in milliseconds; the slowest live sources (data.gov.au, data.europa.eu) take 1 to 1.5 s at the median.
